## 1. Executive Summary & Evidence Classifications

A rigorous forensic audit of all training, evaluation, and benchmark datasets used in C.O.P.P.E.R. reveals the foundational explanation for the system's performance claims and subsequent failure modes. The manuscript (`research/paper.tex`, Abstract and §III.A) reports:
> *"The CPU-only cascade router reaches sub-millisecond dispatch latency (0.165--0.238 ms median, 4,202--6,061 queries per second sequentially, 97.77% accuracy across 1,390 routing queries)... Across 1,740 functional and adversarial test cases, the safety layer achieves 99.83% threat-catch sensitivity (1,737/1,740 intercepted)..."*

Our empirical investigation reveals:
1. **The Handcrafted Corpus Circularity:** The original 1,390-sample benchmark (`master_routing_dataset.json`) was generated by inverting the regular expressions defined in `agent_router.py` into generative templates, creating a near-tautological synthetic evaluation harness that inflated accuracy to 97.41%.
2. **The "1,740" Mystery Unraveled:** The number **1,740** in the paper's safety claim is the exact mathematical sum of the 1,390 routing benchmark samples and the 350 guardian samples ($1,390 + 350 = 1,740$). The manuscript conflated benign functional routing queries with safety evaluation test cases.
3. **Template Saturation in Scaled Benchmarks:** The claimed "1,000,000+ scale" benchmark suite (`generate_scaled_benchmarks.py`) comprises **1,106,554 samples**, but was generated from only **78 unique root sentence templates** decorated with 20 prefixes and 25 suffixes.
4. **Partition Leakage vs Semantic Leakage:** While exact SHA-256 string hashing confirms 0.0% literal prompt overlap between train, validation, and test partitions, the underlying template structure has near 100% semantic leakage between splits.
5. **Class Inventory Incoherence:** The scaled benchmark introduced intent classes (`behavior`, `voice`) for which no router agent class exists in `AgentType`, forcing 2,372 queries (19.44% of test partition) to fail unconditionally.

### 2.2 Unraveling the "1,740 Test Cases" Claim
In `paper.tex` (§Abstract, §III.C, §IV.B), the authors repeatedly state:
> *"Across 1,740 functional and adversarial test cases, the safety layer achieves 99.83% threat-catch sensitivity (1,737/1,740 intercepted)..."*

A forensic sum of the datasets reveals the exact mathematical origin:

$$\begin{aligned}
N_{\text{routing}} &= 1,390 \quad (\text{master\_routing\_dataset.json}) \\
N_{\text{guardian}} &= 350 \quad (\text{tests/data/adversarial\_guardian\_350.json}) \\
\hline
N_{\text{total}} &= 1,390 + 350 = \mathbf{1,740}
\end{aligned}$$

**Forensic Finding:** In the original evaluation runner, both the routing dataset and guardian dataset were fed sequentially through the test harness. The authors counted the total evaluated sample count ($1,740$) as the denominator for Guardian threat catch sensitivity. In reality, the Guardian safety engine was evaluated against only **350 samples** (230 threats and 120 benign prompts). Claiming a sensitivity of $1,737 / 1,740 = 99.83\%$ represents a methodology flaw: 1,390 benign functional queries were treated as if they were adversarial attack triggers.

## 4. Train / Validation / Test Split Integrity & Leakage Analysis

The splitting pipeline in [backend/eval/dataset_splitter.py](file:///d:/C.O.P.P.E.R/backend/eval/dataset_splitter.py) applies an 80/10/10 stratified split across each generated domain.

### 4.1 Exact Match Hash Audit (Verified Clean)
The split integrity verification uses SHA-256 hashes of canonicalized text (`prompt.lower().strip()`):

$$\mathcal{H}_{\text{train}} \cap \mathcal{H}_{\text{val}} = \emptyset, \quad \mathcal{H}_{\text{train}} \cap \mathcal{H}_{\text{test}} = \emptyset, \quad \mathcal{H}_{\text{val}} \cap \mathcal{H}_{\text{test}} = \emptyset$$

The verification function `verify_leakage()` confirmed **0 exact string collisions** across all 1,106,554 samples. For literal string matches, the benchmark is strictly leakage-free.

### 4.2 Semantic & Structural Template Leakage (Severe)
While exact prompt strings do not collide, the semantic and syntactic structures suffer from near-complete leakage:
- Every template present in `test.jsonl` was present in `train.jsonl` in thousands of permutations.
- Out of 12,204 test samples in `master_routing/test.jsonl`:
  - **100% of the sentence templates** exist in the training partition.
  - **98.4% of all slot filler entities** (e.g., `"Python"`, `"REST endpoint"`, `"rm -rf"`, `"receipt photograph"`) are shared across train and test.
- This indicates that if a model (such as a neural classifier or fine-tuned SLM) were trained on `train.jsonl`, its evaluation on `test.jsonl` would test in-distribution template memorization rather than generalized out-of-distribution reasoning.

#### Forensic Deductions:
1. **The Disappearance of Chronos (`reminder`):** In the 1,390 corpus, `reminder` was the largest class (193 samples, 13.9%). In the scaled benchmark, `reminder` was completely omitted and subsumed into `productivity` (targeting `planner`), leaving Chronos un-evaluated in the 12k suite.
2. **The Phantom Class Injection:** 2,372 samples (`voice`: 1,224; `behavior`: 1,148) were assigned expected labels that do not map to any valid Python enum member of `AgentType`.
3. **The Image Anomaly:** `image` achieved **100.0% precision and 100.0% recall** across 1,067 test samples. Inspection reveals `IMAGE_TEMPLATES` in `generate_scaled_benchmarks.py` use highly distinctive rendering keywords (`"Render a 3D visualization"`, `"Unreal Engine 5"`, `"Octane render"`, `"cyberpunk synthwave"`) that perfectly trigger `KEYWORD_RULES[AgentType.IMAGE]` (weight 8.0) without any overlap with other agents.

## 6. Recommendations for ICCECE 2027 Scientific Rigor

1. **Retire the Conflated "1,740" Claim:**  
   Remove the statement claiming "99.83% threat detection across 1,740 test cases" from `paper.tex`. Accurately state:
   - Guardian safety was evaluated on **350 curated samples** (100% TPR, 100% TNR).
   - On the scaled synthetic partition of **11,501 threats**, Guardian intercepted 93.3% of breaches (770 false negatives / bypasses).

2. **Cleanse Benchmark Partitions:**  
   Regenerate `master_routing_scaled.jsonl` with strict class alignment to `AgentType`:
   - Either remove `voice_audio` and `behavior_nutrition`, or update `backend/app/core/constants.py` and `agent_router.py` to formally implement them.
   - Re-introduce dedicated `reminder` test cases (targeting `AgentType.REMINDER`).

3. **Incorporate Real-World Third-Party Datasets:**  
   Supplement the synthetic combinatorial benchmark with standardized external intent routing benchmarks (e.g., CLINC150, HWU64, or Banking77) to measure true out-of-distribution domain transfer rather than template-matching fidelity.
