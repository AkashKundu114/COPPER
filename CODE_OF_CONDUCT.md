# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors, participants, and maintainers of **C.O.P.P.E.R.** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and respectful environment.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, architectural discussions, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback
* Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for individual contributors, but for the stability, safety, and security of the overall project

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, or political vitriol
* Public or private harassment of any participant
* Publishing others' private information (doxing), such as a physical address, private email, or credentials, without explicit permission
* Malicious submission of adversarial exploits, backdoors, or vulnerability reports outside of the designated [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) channels
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open collaboration setting

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The project author and maintainer, **Akash Kundu**, is responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, harmful, or detrimental to the project.

The maintainer has the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, pull requests, issues, and discussions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, and project discussions), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
